Questions

Before you get in touch

Something not covered here? Ask on WhatsApp.

Can I consult over phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. You can call or send a WhatsApp message to describe your matter briefly. We will reply to arrange a consultation by phone, video call or in person at the office.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Bring any documents connected to the matter, such as notices, agreements, sale deeds, court papers, bank statements or correspondence, along with a short timeline of events and a government-issued ID.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Yes. Communications between you and your advocate are privileged and kept confidential, including messages sent while arranging a consultation.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the nature of the matter, the forum and the work involved. They are explained to you clearly before you decide to engage the firm.
